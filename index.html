WHY LIGHT-CURED LASHES?

Light-cured lash technology gives your artist precise control over when each lash attachment cures. Unlike traditional lash adhesives that depend heavily on environmental conditions such as humidity and temperature, the adhesive remains workable until it is cured with targeted light.

The result is a controlled, consistent application process designed around precision, comfort, and beautiful results.

  • PRECISION CURING

    Cured on command for greater control during application.

  • CONSISTENT APPLICATION

    Less dependent on changing temperature and humidity.

  • SAFETY-TESTED TECHNOLOGY

    Independently tested to recognized photobiological safety standards.
