About

Hello, I’mTracie Merricks

I am the owner of Blended Beauty Lounge, a licensed esthetician, and multi-certified in the art of luxury lash extensions. I stay current with the latest techniques and the highest safety practices so every client receives excellent care and exceptional results.

I am driven by a passion for enhancing beauty and well-being. Every lash extension I create is more than a detail-oriented task. It is a therapeutic art form where discipline and concentration come together to make something beautiful.

My mission is to make each client feel special, comforted, and treasured, and to turn every appointment into a moment of serene indulgence and personal connection.

I strive to touch lives in a positive way, so that every visit to my studio leaves a lasting impression of joy, care, and an uplifted spirit.
