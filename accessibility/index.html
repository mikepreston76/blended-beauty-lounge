Accessibility options

Accessibility Statement

Blended Beauty Lounge is committed to making our website accessible to everyone. We want our clients to be able to browse, learn about our services, and book appointments with ease, whether they use a screen reader, keyboard navigation, larger text, or other assistive technology.

We continue to review and improve the accessibility of our site. This includes clear headings, meaningful alt text, visible focus states, readable contrast, and support for keyboard and zoomed browsing.

Accessibility features

  • Skip links to quickly move to the main content
  • Keyboard-friendly navigation and visible focus styles
  • Text sizing and contrast tools built into the site
  • Reduced-motion support for users who prefer less animation
  • Clear link text and descriptive alternative text for meaningful images

Accessibility needs

If you have trouble using our website because of a disability, or if you need help scheduling an appointment, please contact us. We will do our best to help by phone, email, or in person.
