Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Blended Beauty Lounge respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, or other information you provide when contacting us or requesting information about our services.

Our website and third-party services may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and general website usage information.

How We Use Your Information

We may use information collected to:

  • Respond to questions or requests
  • Provide information about our services
  • Schedule or facilitate appointments
  • Improve our website and customer experience
  • Understand how visitors use our website
  • Communicate with clients when appropriate
  • Maintain the security and functionality of our website

Online Booking

Appointment scheduling and certain related services may be provided through Square. When you follow a link to Square or provide information through its services, your information is subject to Square's privacy policy and terms.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand website traffic, improve performance, and provide a better browsing experience.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may function differently when cookies are disabled.

Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to or use services provided by third parties, including Square, social media platforms, analytics providers, maps, and other website services.

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies and practices. Blended Beauty Lounge is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

Sharing of Personal Information

Blended Beauty Lounge does not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website, provide requested services, process appointments, or support our business operations. We may also disclose information when required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13.

Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about personal information you have provided directly to Blended Beauty Lounge or to request that we correct or delete that information, subject to applicable legal and business recordkeeping requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our website, services, technology, or legal requirements. The updated date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was most recently revised.
